**Zero em dashes (—).** Not in website copy, not in documents, not in chat replies to
him. Use commas, periods, or a colon before a list. His words, 2026-07-29: "il y a des
tirets qui font IA sur le site de alictro... Je ne veux plus jamais ça. Je veux qu'on
enlève tout ce qui fait générique IA."

Also cut generic AI marketing vocabulary: seamless, robust, leverage, elevate,
transformative, streamline, unlock, empower, cutting-edge, harness, delve. And hollow
parallel slogans like "Real projects, real results" that sound rhythmic but say nothing.

**Why:** he sends this work to real business owners to win contracts. Copy that reads as
machine-generated undermines the pitch, and he noticed the dashes himself before any
client did. The first Alictro build shipped with 38 of them.

**How to apply:** invoke the `no-ai-slop` skill on any marketing or client-facing copy,
in both English and French, then grep for `—` and expect zero. Watch my own chat
register too, not just the artifact. Related: [[prospect-demo-workflow]] and
[[demo-delivery-single-html]].
